Privacy policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026
In short
  • FitBoss has no accounts, no servers of its own, no ads and no analytics.
  • Your plan, workout logs and measurements are stored only on your device. Jacques Gagelin does not receive or see them.
  • The only thing that goes outside is the Pro payment, which is handled by Google Play.
  • You can erase everything from Settings or by uninstalling the app.

01Who is responsible

FitBoss (“the app”) is published by Jacques Gagelin. For any question about this policy write to fitbossapp@gmail.com.

02What the app stores on your device

To build your plan and track your progress, the app keeps in your device's private storage what you type or choose:

DataPurpose
Name or nickname, age, weight, heightCalculate your plan and suggested loads.
Goal, level, available days, equipmentChoose exercises and organise your week.
Workout logs (sets, loads, duration, perceived effort, heart rate or calories if you enter them)Show your progress and feed the game.
Body measurements you logProgress charts.
Game progress, points and settings (units, theme)Keep your campaign and preferences.
Whether Pro is active on your Google accountUnlock paid features.

This data never leaves your device through the app: nothing is sent to servers, there is no cloud sync of our own, and Jacques Gagelin has no access to it. Weight, measurements and heart-rate figures are health and wellness data that you choose to enter; the app handles them locally only.

03What we do not do

  • We do not create accounts or ask for an email, phone number or password.
  • We use no analytics, trackers, advertising or third-party tracking tools.
  • We do not sell, rent or share data, because we do not receive any.
  • We do not request camera, microphone, location, contacts, file or health-sensor permissions.

04Purchases and Google Play

Pro is billed through Google Play (monthly subscription or one-time purchase). Payment, your payment method and billing details are handled by Google under its own Privacy Policy; FitBoss never sees them. The app only asks Google Play whether your account has an active Pro purchase in order to unlock or lock features. You can cancel the subscription at any time in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions.

05Links to other sites

Some exercises include a link to a YouTube video search. Tapping it opens outside the app (in your browser or the YouTube app), where YouTube's and Google's policies apply. FitBoss does not send any information about you with that link.

06Backups and export

From Settings you can copy your data as text and restore it on another device; that copy stays wherever you paste it (notes, email, etc.) and is your responsibility. Also, if Android's backup is turned on, the system itself may include the app's data in the backup it keeps on your Google account; that is managed by Android and you can turn it off in your phone's settings.

07Your control and data deletion

You are in full control of your data: you can edit it in the app, export it from Settings, and erase everything with the delete option in Settings. Uninstalling the app also removes the local data (except any copy Android may have backed up, as described above). Because FitBoss keeps no data about you outside your device, there is nothing for us to delete on request.

08Children

FitBoss is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them. If you are a parent or guardian and have questions, please contact us.

09Health notice

FitBoss does not provide medical advice. Plans and suggested loads are guidance only. If you have a health condition, are pregnant, are recovering from an injury or have not trained for a long time, consult a professional before starting. Stop exercising if you feel pain, dizziness or shortness of breath.

10Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy (for example, if it ever adds accounts or sync), we will update this page, change the date above and tell you inside the app before collecting any new data.
